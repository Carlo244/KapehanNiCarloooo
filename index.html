Hey There! Welcome to Kapehan ni Carloooo!

Get ready to experience coffee like never before! At Kapehan ni Carloooo, we're absolutely obsessed with coffee, and we're thrilled to share that passion with you. Come on in and discover your new favorite cup in our cozy and inviting space.

What Makes Our Coffee (and Us!) So Awesome?

We're super picky about our coffee! We handpick the best locally sourced beans, treat them with love, and our amazing baristas work their magic to brew you the most delicious coffee imaginable. But it's not just about the coffee – we've built a place where you can kick back, chat with friends, and just enjoy the good vibes.

You HAVE to Try These! (Our Best Sellers)

  • The Carloooo Special Espresso: Seriously smooth, incredibly bold, and totally unforgettable. You'll be hooked!
  • Zen Matcha Dream Latte: Imagine creamy perfection with a calming touch of zen. It's like a hug in a mug!
  • Our Famous Homemade Cheesecake: Oh. My. Goodness. Every bite is pure heaven. Trust us on this one!

Where It All Began...

The idea for Kapehan ni Carloooo sparked from a simple love for coffee and a desire to create a place where everyone feels welcome. Back in 2020, we took the leap, and we've been pouring our hearts (and lots of coffee!) into making this dream a reality ever since. We can't wait to share it with you!
